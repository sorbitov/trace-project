--- Slide 3 ---
PROBLEM
Crypto payments still start with an address request
Most Web3 coordination happens around social identity. Payment still forces the recipient to share a wallet address before the sender can act.
DM friction and delayed payouts
Copy/paste address risk
No safe direct transfer when the wallet is unknown
TRACE changes the destination from a wallet address to a verified recipient identity.

--- Slide 4 ---
SOLUTION FLOW
TRACE turns an X account into a claimable USDC destination
Sender enters X handle
USDC funds Arc escrow
Recipient proves X + wallet
Claim, cancel or refund settles onchain
The backend cannot mark funding or settlement complete from a client claim alone; it verifies Arc receipts and matching contract events.

--- Slide 7 ---
SECURITY MODEL
Security centers on proof binding and recovery
Recipient proof
Fresh X OAuth must match the immutable recipient X user ID.
Wallet proof
A one-time signed challenge binds the destination wallet to the session.
Claim digest
Authorization binds payment ID, wallet, chain ID, contract and expiry.
Recovery
Sender cancel/refund remains available under contract policy.
Privacy boundary
Unauthorized API views omit amount, sender and memo; chain activity remains public.
Production gap
External contract/backend review and managed key custody remain required.
